# Submissions

Journal- and venue-specific manuscript drafts, cover letters, review responses,
and audit notes that previously lived at the repository root. They are kept
here for provenance but are not part of the R package build (they are listed
in `.Rbuildignore`).

Layout:

- `F1000_*.md` — F1000Research software-tool submission package.
- `PLOS_ONE_*.md` — PLOS ONE manuscript drafts and reviews.
- `RSM_*.md` — Research Synthesis Methods submission.
- `E156-PROTOCOL.md` — E156 micro-paper protocol (see also `../e156-submission/`).
- `HTA_IMPACT_REPORT.md`, `IMPACT_ASSESSMENT_REVIEW.md`,
  `MULTIPERSON_REVIEW.md`, `RED_TEAM_AUDIT.md`, `SOLUTION_README.md` —
  internal reviews and impact write-ups.
